OUR STORY

Building homes in Tirupur and Coimbatore since 1996.

JS Groups began with residential plots and grew into building premium independent villas in gated communities. Founded by Mr. K. J. Prabakaran and Mr. J. Anandkumar, we have delivered over 25 projects and served more than 1,000 families. Quality, trust and transparency guide every home we build. Mr. J. Anandkumar is also an active member of prestigious professional and service organisations, including Rotary, BNI and BAI, reflecting his commitment to professional excellence, networking and community development.

JS Group brings together and oversees all its project companies under one unified organisation, ensuring smooth coordination, professional management and consistent standards across every project. While each company independently promotes and manages its developments, JS Group provides the overall leadership, guidance and organisational support, upholding the values of quality, trust and transparency in every endeavour.
